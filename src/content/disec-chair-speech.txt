Good morning, Respected Secretary-General and the chairperson of TESMUN 2026,Mr. Dibas Khadka, Honorable Chief Guest and Managing Director Mr. Ram Basnet; our Special Guest and the Chairperson of our school Madam Sheetal Rana; Respected Principal, Mr. Prashant Rajak; Administrative In-charge, Mr. Neeraj Bikram Rana; and our dedicated MUN Advisor, Mr. Govinda Gautam. I also extend my gratitude to the Executive Board, the Organizing Committee, Press, IT, and Logistics teams, our supervisors, in-charges, teachers, my fellow dais members, and, above all, the brilliant delegates of TESMUN 2026.
Standing on this stage today as the Chair of DISEC is a deeply personal full-circle moment. My MUN journey began right in a Legislative Parliament simulation, where I represented Upendra Yadav. Back then, I was terrified, my hands trembled, my voice cracked, and I barely managed to speak. But I refused to let fear define me. The following year, I stepped into DISEC as the delegate of Iran. I found my ground, spoke up with conviction, and earned a Special Mention. Now, sitting on the other side of the table as your Chair, I realize that true growth in MUN doesn't happen when you win an award; it happens the moment you overcome the fear of raising your placard.
Stepping up to run the committee gave me a front-row seat to an incredible display of talent. Instead of worrying about my own motions or foreign policy, my job was to direct the momentum, keep the clock ticking, and ensure every single nation had a fair shot at the floor. It was a massive responsibility, but sharing the Dais alongside my Vice Chair, Mr. Rijan Shrestha, and my Moderator, Mr. Prasanna Budhathoki, made the pressure entirely worth it. To my team: thank you for keeping order in the court, stepping up through every debate, and making this experience unforgettable.
And what an incredible, cinematic committee room we had. From the very first roll call, the USA and Russia were locked in a non-stop debate battle, ready to argue over literally anything. I’m pretty sure if one said the sky was blue, the other would file a formal protest. China mastered the art of quiet intimidation, speaking barely above a whisper, yet dropping points so sharp that the entire room instantly went silent. Japan decided that the best diplomatic strategy was pure volume and maximum energy for every single motion, while the UK sat back, dropped a couple of logical facts, and casually disassembled everyone else’s arguments.Then we had Belarus, who spent half the conference practically begging the Dias for just five seconds on the speaker's list, and Sudan, who walked around looking completely confused about which planet, let alone which country, they were supposed to be representing.
As your Chair, watching your evolution over these past few days was the ultimate reward. I felt every spike of tension during unmoderated caucuses, every triumph when a draft resolution came together, and every spark of pride when a delegate conquered their fear at the podium. You did not just participate in this conference, you breathed life into it.
As this chapter closes, I know I will deeply miss every single second of this role from tapping the gavel to navigating the chaos inside the committee room. Yet, as hard as it is to step down, I am genuinely thrilled for the future. I have no doubt that upcoming batches will carry this torch even higher, exceed our achievements, and keep MUN a sacred, transformative institution for our school.
Now, the moment we have all been waiting for, the Award Distribution for the DISEC Committee.
I would like to invite our Committee In-charge, Mr. Bidur Lamichhane sir, to the stage to confer the certificates to our distinguished delegates, along with my Vice Chair, Mr. Rijan Shrestha, and Moderator, Mr. Prasanna Budhathoki, as they present our initial set of awards.
Whether your name was called today or not, remember this: an award is just a piece of paper, but the confidence, articulate voice, and resilience you built in that room will stay with you forever. Do not be discouraged; let this be the fuel that drives you to return stronger, bolder, and more prepared next time.
Thank you, delegates. Thank you, TESMUN 2026, for memories that will last a lifetime.
