“MUN ends here, but the voice we found here carries forward.”
Honourable Secretary General Mr. Dibas Khadka, Honourable Chief Guest Mr. Ram Basnet, Chairperson Madam Sheetal Rana, Administrative In-charge Mr. Neeraj Bikram Rana, Principal Mr. Prashant Rajak, our MUN Coordinator Mr. Govinda Gautam, esteemed dais members, logistics, press, and IT teams, and all the distinguished delegates—greetings to everyone.
I, Aakarshi Paudel, stand before you today, filled with deep pride and honour.
My journey with MUN began in Grade 7, when I first joined as an interviewer. Interviewing the dais members and delegates gave me my first real glimpse into what MUN was all about. Then, in Grade 8, I returned as a delegate, representing Rabi Lamichhane from the Rashtriya Swatantra Party, and I was fortunate enough to receive the position of Outstanding Delegate. But for me, Outstanding Delegate was not the end. I wanted to challenge myself further.
In the International Committee, I was assigned to the United States of America in DISEC. I represented my country with confidence and determination, and that journey led me to receive Best Delegate.
And today, standing before you as the Chair of Model Parliament 1, I truly feel honoured. More than anything, I feel proud of the younger Aakarshi who once dreamed of achieving something bigger within this school.
I will admit—I was extremely nervous walking into MP 1 for the first time. But the moment I saw the enthusiasm of my delegates, that nervousness slowly turned into excitement. Throughout the conference, I witnessed passionate debates, thoughtful arguments, remarkable collaboration, and, of course, a few moments that I know I will remember for a very long time.
What I witnessed was a transformation taking place. The committee, which began rather quietly, slowly found its voice and gave real force to the debates. I will forever cherish the powerful voice of Gyan Bahadur Shahi, the confidence and presence of Rabi Lamichhane, the calm yet logical arguments of our outstanding delegate Manish Jha, and Ambika Devi asking me, “Chair, this! Chair, that!” every few minutes. And how could I forget Arjun Nar, who somehow managed to go beyond the agenda while still having something to say about everything? Also how can i forget the times i being shadow delegate and betraying the rsp and joining the opposition party. Screaming to the top of my lungs, banging the table and shouting and questioning being a press. Sometimes I even felt like I was a chair or a delegate or a press member.
But jokes aside, I will genuinely cherish every single delegate of MP 1. Each of you brought something different to this committee, and watching you grow throughout these sessions has been one of the most rewarding parts of my MUN journey.
I would also like to express my heartfelt gratitude to our Secretary General, especially for stepping in and supporting the committee during some of my most overwhelming moments. I am truly grateful to the Deputy Secretary General, logistics team, IT team, and press team for their constant support throughout the conference.
And of course, I must thank my wonderful colleagues, Vice Chair Ms Dilasha Oli and Moderator Mr Sijan Neupane, for standing beside me and supporting me throughout this entire journey.
Thank you, delegates, for making MP 1 a committee I will always remember.
And now, I would like to call upon our Committee In-Charge, Mr. Bipin Koirala, to kindly come forward and present the certificates to our amazing delegates. Following this, I would like to hand over the stage to our Vice Chair, Ms. Dilasha Oli, and Moderator, Mr. Sijan Neupane, for the announcement of the awardees. 
Thank you.
