Good morning and Namaskar to everyone present here.

Chairperson of TESMUN 2083, Secretary General Mr. Dibas Khadka, Honorable Chief Guest and Managing Director Mr. Ram Basnet, Special Guest and Chairperson of TES Madam Sheetal Rana, Administrative In-Charge Mr. Niraj Bikram Rana, distinguished supervisors and in-charges, esteemed teachers, dedicated staff members, and, most importantly, our young delegates.

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you all to TESMUN 2026.

Today also carries a painful significance for Nepal. Bhadra 16 remains a day of grief in our national memory, associated with the tragic killing of twelve Nepali citizens in Iraq and the profound sorrow and unrest that followed in Nepal.

We remember this history not to reopen wounds or assign blame, but to remind ourselves of what happens when human lives are lost amid conflict, when communication breaks down, and when grief and anger overwhelm understanding. And that is precisely why a Model United Nations conference carries meaning.

TESMUN is not merely about debating international issues. It is about practicing the skills the world needs when disagreements become difficult: dialogue, diplomacy, negotiation, empathy, international cooperation, and responsible decision-making.

Look at the world around us today. Geopolitical tensions continue to affect international relationships. Wars and humanitarian crises continue to displace communities. Climate change is placing increasing pressure on vulnerable societies. Technology and artificial intelligence are transforming economies, education, security, and communication.

And Nepal is not separate from these global realities. Our Himalayan environment makes us particularly vulnerable to climate-related risks. Migration, economic opportunity, regional relations, development, governance, and disaster resilience are all connected to the larger international system.

The world therefore needs more than powerful voices. It needs thoughtful voices, listening minds, and responsible leaders.

Here, you will represent nations whose interests may be very different from your own. You will encounter arguments that challenge your assumptions and negotiate with people who may initially disagree with you. And that is exactly the learning we want.

Diplomacy is not about making everyone think alike. It is about finding a way forward when we do not.

You will learn that a strong argument needs evidence, a good diplomat needs empathy, and a meaningful resolution requires cooperation.

This connects directly with what we strive to nurture at The Excelsior School. We want students who are emotionally intelligent, ethical, responsible, confident, compassionate, creative, critical thinkers, and future-ready leaders. We do not want our students simply to inherit the world as it is. We want them to have the courage, competence, and character to improve it.

So, dear delegates, as you enter your committees, carry three principles with you: listen deeply, think critically, and lead responsibly.

Speak with confidence, but listen with humility. Defend your country’s position, but remain open to another perspective. Seek solutions, not simply victories.

You are not simply attending a conference; you are rehearsing for the world. The skills you practice here—negotiation, communication, critical thinking, collaboration, and ethical decision-making—will extend far beyond these committee rooms.

On a day that carries remembrance, let us begin TESMUN with a shared commitment: may we remember without hatred, disagree without disrespect, and negotiate without losing our humanity.

You may not change the world during TESMUN. But you may change a conversation, change your perspective, or inspire an idea. Sometimes, that is how change begins.

With immense pride and optimism, I welcome you all to TESMUN 2026. May these days challenge your thinking, broaden your horizons, strengthen your voice, and inspire you to lead with wisdom, empathy, integrity, and purpose.

With that, it is my privilege to officially declare TESMUN 2026 open.

Thank you, and I wish every delegate an inspiring, meaningful, and transformative conference.
