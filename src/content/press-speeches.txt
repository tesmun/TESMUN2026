UNEP Chair

Honourable Secretary-General Mr. Dibas Khadka, Chief Guest Mr. Ram Basnet, Chairperson Madam Sheetal Rana, Administrative In-charge Mr. Neeraj Bikram Rana, Principal Mr. Prashant Rajak, MUN Advisor Mr. Govinda Gautam, fellow dais members, teams, and delegates of UNEP—good afternoon.

After days of debate, negotiations, accusations, rebuttals, points of information, questionable statistics, and suspensions, we have reached the end of UNEP. China and Iran kept the committee engaged, Bangladesh brought confidence and presence, Germany reminded everyone that it had money, and Kenya and Canada became familiar with suspensions. Afghanistan kept everyone on their toes, Nepal gave us memorable moments, and every delegate made the committee what it was.

UNEP was never just about countries. It was about people. I watched nervous delegates become confident, disagreements turn into negotiations, and arguments turn into diplomacy. MUN is not about having the perfect speech; it is about having the courage to speak, listen, defend an idea, and sometimes admit that you might be wrong.

I hope you remember that MUN gives us a voice. Continue to use that voice long after this conference is over. Thank you for making UNEP difficult, chaotic, hilarious, and worth remembering. It has been an honour to serve as your Chair. The United Nations Environment Programme at TESMUN 2026 is hereby adjourned.

HRC Chair

Good morning everyone. As Chair of the Human Rights Council, I do not know whether to call this conference a diplomatic success or a three-day survival challenge. We had delegates from Israel and Palestine, and Russia and Ukraine, who sometimes seemed to need a history lesson. China and Indonesia brought memorable moments, Qatar became the committee’s favourite punchline, Syria’s speeches surprised the council, and the pages kept us entertained.

Japan found a new excuse for everything, Nigeria found a new place to sit, and the USA and DPRK were among the smartest delegates in the room. Jokes aside, thank you for debating, questioning, disagreeing, and making this committee memorable. Behind the chaos, we discussed real issues involving real people, and I hope everyone leaves with a better understanding of the responsibility that comes with discussing human rights.

To my Executive Board, delegates, pages, and everyone who made this conference possible, thank you. This council may be ending, but I will remember the chaos, laughter, arguments, and every one of you. Thank you for the memories and for making my experience as Chair unforgettable.
