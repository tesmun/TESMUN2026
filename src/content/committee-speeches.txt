LP I Chair — Aakarshi Paudel

MUN ends here, but the voice we found here carries forward.

Honourable Secretary-General Mr. Dibas Khadka, Honourable Chief Guest Mr. Ram Basnet, Chairperson Madam Sheetal Rana, Administrative In-charge Mr. Neeraj Bikram Rana, Principal Mr. Prashant Rajak, our MUN Coordinator Mr. Govinda Gautam, esteemed dais members, logistics, press, and IT teams, and all the distinguished delegates—greetings to everyone.

I, Aakarshi Paudel, stand before you today filled with deep pride and honour. My journey with MUN began in Grade 7, when I first joined as an interviewer. In Grade 8, I returned as a delegate representing Rabi Lamichhane and was fortunate to receive Outstanding Delegate. In DISEC, I represented the United States of America and received Best Delegate.

Today, standing before you as Chair of Model Parliament 1, I feel truly honoured. I witnessed passionate debates, thoughtful arguments, remarkable collaboration, and the transformation of a quiet committee into one that found its voice.

I will cherish every delegate of MP 1. Each of you brought something different, and watching you grow was one of the most rewarding parts of my MUN journey. Thank you to the Secretariat, logistics, IT, press, my colleagues Vice Chair Dilasha Oli and Moderator Sijan Neupane, and every person who supported this committee.

Thank you, delegates, for making MP 1 a committee I will always remember.

ECOSOC Chair

A very warm good morning to the Secretary-General Mr. Dibas Khadka, Managing Director and Chief Guest Mr. Ram Basnet, Chairperson and Special Guest Madam Sheetal Rana, Principal Mr. Prashant Rajak, Administrative In-charge Mr. Niraj Bikram Rana, MUN Advisor Mr. Govinda Gautam, distinguished members of the Executive Board, Organizing Committee, delegates, and everyone present here today.

Three years ago, I walked into my first MUN as a delegate. Today, standing here as Chair of ECOSOC, I find myself looking back at how much has changed. My TESMUN journey began in Grade 8 in Legislative Parliament, where I represented Ramesh Lekhak and received a Special Mention. The following year, I returned to ECOSOC as China and received Best Delegate.

As a Chair, you see the speeches, negotiations, arguments, alliances, and unexpected moments that bring a committee together. I had the privilege of chairing ECOSOC alongside Vice Chair Sima Thapa and Moderator Kunjal Karki.

To the delegates, I hope you take more than awards from this conference. Take the confidence to speak up, the ability to listen to people you disagree with, and the memories you made along the way. Thank you to the Executive Board, Organizing Committee, and everyone who made TESMUN 2026 possible.
