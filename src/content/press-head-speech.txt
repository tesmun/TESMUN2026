As Chair of the Human Rights Council, I honestly don't know whether to call this conference a diplomatic success or a three-day survival challenge.We had delegates from Israel and Palestine who sometimes seemed to forget why the war in their own countries happened, and Russia and Ukraine doing the same. Apparently, even the countries involved needed a history lesson.
Then came our superheroes, China and Indonesia.
We heard the legendary, “With great power comes great responsibility” from Captain China, followed by Indonesia's unforgettable, “You become what you eat, so China is a dog.”
And of course, Qatar, you will forever remain this committee's favourite pee-john. Syria's speeches were basically jumpscares for the entire council. Our pages kept us entertained with their extremely unfunny elephant and fruit jokes.
Then there was Japan, with a new excuse for everything, to the point where I started seeing Japan becoming President in my dreams.My Vice Chair was constantly crashing out, while my Moderator spent the entire session trying to act chill while everything was falling apart.
Nigeria somehow found a new place to sit every day to deliver better speeches. UK, thank you for keeping all my secrets. And DPRK, along with USA, you were genuinely among the smartest delegates in the room.
But jokes aside, to my lovely council, thank you for debating, questioning, disagreeing, and making this committee memorable. Behind all the chaos, we discussed real issues involving real people, and I hope everyone leaves with a better understanding of the responsibility that comes with discussing human rights. I am glad to be the leader of HRC as said by the fellow member of HRC , Nepal.
