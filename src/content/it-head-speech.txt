My pleasures have no bound as I stand before you addressing this assembly. During these three days a lot of ups and downs, hustles and bustles went on. There were deadlines to meet, people to have interaction with, and tasks to be accomplished. We came, we gathered, we did. What we saw: the beautiful banners, the wonderful webpages, and vivid videos are all the toils of my team mates. They killed their sleep, took tasks in hand and made the difference. We talked, we worked and we learned. This is how life grows and I am happy that we are growing. 
Growth is a continuous process. Let me recall mine. My TESMUN journey began in Grade 8, in the Legislative Parliament, where I represented Gagan Kumar Thapa and received a Special Mention. The following year, I returned to UNEP as the delegate of China and received the Outstanding Delegate award.
At that stage, MUN was largely about preparation. Researching, building arguments, delivering speeches, and trying to perform better with every conference.
But as I went through more conferences, I began to understand that MUN teaches something much larger than parliamentary procedure.
It teaches you to enter a room with a position, and still have the humility to listen. It teaches you to defend an argument without becoming attached to it, to disagree without becoming disrespectful, and to understand that diplomacy is not about having the loudest voice in the room. It is about knowing how to make your voice matter.
This year, I had the opportunity to experience TESMUN from an entirely different perspective.
My work was largely behind the scenes, from the website and conference graphics to the opening and closing videos. But that role showed me something I had never fully appreciated as a delegate.
A conference that feels seamless from the audience is rarely seamless behind the scenes.
There is planning, coordination, constant revision, and a great deal of work that only becomes visible when something goes wrong. And through that process, I saw just how many people contribute to making these few days possible.
That is perhaps what I will remember most about TESMUN 2026.
To the delegates, I hope you take that perspective with you. Challenge ideas. Question assumptions. Listen carefully. Speak with purpose. And do not be afraid to step into roles that initially seem unfamiliar. You must have realized now that your long journey of growth has just begun. Congratulations! 
Once more, thank you, and congratulations to everyone who made TESMUN 2026 a success.
